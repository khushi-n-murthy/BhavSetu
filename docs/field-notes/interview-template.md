# Owner interview template

Ask for consent before you start. Record only what the interviewee agrees to share. No unit names without written consent; blur faces, name boards and number plates in photos.

- Date:
- Cluster (Peenya / Bommasandra / other):
- Sector (fabrication / plastics / garments / components):
- Size (people, rough turnover band):
- Consent to quote anonymously: yes / no
- Consent to name the unit: yes / no (written)

## The six official questions

1. How many suppliers do you call for a typical purchase, and how long does it take? (count, hours, channel)
2. How are quotes compared, and which costs get missed? (photo of a comparison sheet if allowed; freight, loading, credit, GST, cutting charges)
3. How was the last quote to a buyer worked out? (step by step, with real numbers)
4. How often has an order turned out to be a loss? (count in 12 months, size, cause)
5. Where do new buyer enquiries come from today? (channels, share of revenue from top 3 buyers)
6. Would neighbouring units buy together? (yes/no, conditions, trust issues)

## Our own probes

- Show me the last WhatsApp from a supplier.
- How often did material arrive late in the last six months, and by how many days?
- What did the last delay cost you: buyer penalty, idle workers, overtime, a lost customer?
- Do your buyers' POs carry a late-delivery penalty, and how much?
- What would make you trust a machine to negotiate for you?
- What would you pay a month if it saved you ₹1 lakh a year?
